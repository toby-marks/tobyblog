# Congratulations, you have found something special.

At long last with the help of AI I am finally able to offer you this feature of the site, in the way I want to present it and with all the features I want it to have. 

*The only thing is you'll need to be logged in to Spotify on your desktop to listen to the whole track, otherwise Spotify only gives you 30 seconds. Can't do anything about that. But you can always just follow the link to open it directly in the app — and give it a follow, while you're at it.*

This lovingly curated playlist is, in my opinion, a classic party jam. Some might argue with that, but I think it succeeds in capturing a very specific vibe that is unmistakably "Tobian", despite showing some obvious influences from a few classic playlists, like the [Dinosaur Dracula Halloween Jukebox](https://dinosaurdracula.com/halloween-jukebox/), and featuring a few beloved old classics.

There are some sleepers in here, though. Like Bloodrock's D.O.A., which is probably the scariest song I have ever heard, despite it being about nothing more than the everyday horrors of life.

As always, enjoy.
