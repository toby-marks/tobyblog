Last night around midnight the family gathered around the front door. An ambulance had pulled up. Cops were on the scene. We watched them haul a body out of the rent house three doors down from us. Must have been natural causes, as there were only a couple cop cars and sirens weren't blaring. No crime scene tape. It was wrapped head to toe when they hauled it out of the house. They put the body in an ambulance. They hung around for a while. No rush to the hospital. 

There was a girl there once that sold slime. She was really into Augie's show. Her and her mom.
