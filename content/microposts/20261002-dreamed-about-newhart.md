# A Strange Dream About Bob Newhart

Last night I had another dream about Bob Newhart. For some reason I don't understand I've had more celebrity dreams about him than anyone save Donald Trump.

In the dream I knew him personally, and was at his house demoing a “Newhart” game I’d developed for the Gameboy. But I couldn’t remember his name and kept calling him Dick.

He didn’t mention anything, but I could tell he was annoyed.

In another part of the dream I was on a hunt for treasure on the old family property in Louisiana. Other people were involved in the search that I didn't know.
