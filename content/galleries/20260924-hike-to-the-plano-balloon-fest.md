We parked at our usual spot, about a 25 minute walk from the festival grounds. It was hot, as it usually is, with a high of around 97 that day. I don't mind the walk, though. It gives us time to reminisce on the way over, and time to reflect on the way back.

This year it was just Lucy and me. Augie joined us later. 

{{< figure 
      src="https://imagedelivery.net/zJmFZzaNuqC_Q5Caqyu8nQ/26-09-19909/fit=scale-down,w=1600,sharpen=1,f=auto,q=0.9,slow-connection-quality=0.3"

As we made our way to the ticket booths an old school Evangelical preacher shouted dire warnings to us about the state of our souls.

      src="https://imagedelivery.net/zJmFZzaNuqC_Q5Caqyu8nQ/26-09-19912/fit=scale-down,w=1600,sharpen=1,f=auto,q=0.9,slow-connection-quality=0.3"
      alt=""

The crowds are huge, but there are enough food trucks and drink stations that getting a decent snack doesn't take inordinately long. People are pretty chill, and everybody's having a good time. Augie tells me he read around 100,000 people visit the festival every year.
