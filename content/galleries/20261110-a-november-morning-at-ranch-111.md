As always, it's a beautiful but strange place. Practically no one was there. Just a maintenance truck and a couple of other cars. I noticed only as I was driving away that someone was sitting in that maintenance truck the whole time. As I set out with my camera, taking a few early shots to get oriented, I noticed a guy pull up in his car only a couple of spots over from me in the parking lot, maybe about 60 yards or so from where I was standing. He got out and smoked a cigarette, pacing around a little, then drove off abruptly. Other than that I had the park to myself. 

Was I being watched?
